The project currently has a single maintainer, so required approving reviews
are set to zero: a person cannot usefully approve their own pull request. All
other protections — required checks, required pull requests, and conversation
resolution — still apply. When a second maintainer joins, one approving review
becomes required and CODEOWNER review is enabled for sensitive paths.

## Emeritus

None yet.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no application form. Maintainers are invited after a sustained record
of good contributions and reviews. If you would like to head in that direction,
the most useful things you can do are reviewing other people's pull requests
and helping triage issues.
